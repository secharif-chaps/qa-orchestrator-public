# Creating Company Screenings

Learn how to create new company screening tasks to analyze businesses for risk assessment and due diligence.

## Getting Started

### What is Company Screening?
Company screening is the process of analyzing a business entity to assess various risk factors, compliance status, and business viability. This includes:
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Checking against sanctions lists and regulatory databases
- **Financial Health**: Analyzing financial stability and creditworthiness
- **Reputation Analysis**: Assessing public perception and media coverage
- **Ownership Structure**: Understanding corporate structure and beneficial ownership

## Creating a New Screening

### Step 1: Access the Creation Form
1. Navigate to the Companies section
2. Click the "Create New Screening" button
3. You'll be presented with the screening configuration form

### Step 2: Company Information
Enter the target company details:
- **Company Name**: Full legal name of the entity
- **Registration Number**: Official business registration identifier
- **Country of Incorporation**: Where the company is legally registered
- **Industry Sector**: Business sector or industry classification

### Step 3: Screening Parameters
Configure what aspects to analyze:

#### Risk Assessment Options
- **Sanctions Screening**: Check against global sanctions lists
- **PEP Screening**: Identify Politically Exposed Persons
- **Adverse Media**: Search for negative news and media coverage
- **Financial Analysis**: Assess financial health and stability

#### Screening Depth
- **Basic Screening**: Core compliance and sanctions checks
- **Enhanced Screening**: Comprehensive analysis including beneficial ownership
- **Ongoing Monitoring**: Continuous screening with regular updates

### Step 4: Additional Settings
- **Priority Level**: Set screening urgency (High, Medium, Low)
- **Notification Settings**: Configure alerts for screening completion
- **Report Format**: Choose output format for results

## Understanding Results

### Screening Status
- **In Progress**: Analysis is currently running
- **Completed**: Screening finished successfully
- **Failed**: Technical issues occurred during screening
- **Requires Review**: Manual review needed for complex cases

### Risk Categories
Results are categorized by risk level:
- **High Risk**: Immediate attention required
- **Medium Risk**: Further investigation recommended
- **Low Risk**: Standard monitoring sufficient
- **No Risk Found**: Clean screening results

## Best Practices

### Preparation
- **Accurate Information**: Ensure company details are correct and complete
- **Recent Data**: Use the most current company information available
- **Multiple Identifiers**: Provide various company identifiers when possible

### Follow-up Actions
- **Review Results**: Carefully analyze screening outcomes
- **Document Decisions**: Record actions taken based on screening results
- **Regular Updates**: Schedule periodic re-screening for ongoing monitoring

### Quality Assurance
- **Data Verification**: Cross-check results with primary sources
- **False Positive Review**: Investigate potential false matches
- **Escalation Procedures**: Know when to escalate to compliance experts
