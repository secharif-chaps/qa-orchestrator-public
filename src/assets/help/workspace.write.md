# Workspace Team Management (Write Access)

Manage your workspace team members, roles, and permissions with full administrative control over team composition and access levels.

## Team Management

### Adding New Team Members

#### Invitation Process
1. **Navigate to Team Settings**: Access the workspace team management page
2. **Click "Invite Member"**: Start the invitation process
3. **Enter Member Details**:
   - **Email Address**: Primary contact email
   - **Full Name**: Display name for the workspace
   - **Initial Role**: Set appropriate permission level
4. **Send Invitation**: Member receives email invitation to join
5. **Track Status**: Monitor invitation acceptance and onboarding

#### Role Assignment
Choose appropriate roles based on job function:
- **Workspace Admin**: Full workspace control and team management
- **Manager**: Team oversight and content management
- **Analyst**: Core screening and analysis capabilities
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to screening results
- **Guest**: Limited access to shared content only

### Managing Existing Members

#### Updating Member Information
- **Role Changes**: Promote or modify permission levels
- **Contact Updates**: Update email addresses and display names
- **Status Management**: Activate, deactivate, or suspend members
- **Access Reviews**: Regular audits of member permissions

#### Removing Team Members
1. **Select Member**: Choose the member to remove
2. **Review Dependencies**: Check for active screenings or processes
3. **Transfer Ownership**: Reassign any owned content
4. **Confirm Removal**: Complete the removal process
5. **Access Revocation**: Ensure all system access is terminated

## Permission Management

### Role-Based Access Control

#### Understanding Permission Levels
Each role includes specific capabilities:

**Workspace Admin**
- Full team management authority
- Workspace configuration control
- Billing and subscription management
- Integration and API access management

**Manager**
- Team member oversight
- Content and screening management
- Workflow approval authority
- Reporting and analytics access

**Analyst**
- Company screening creation and management
- Risk assessment and analysis
- Report generation and sharing
- Standard workflow execution

**Viewer**
- Read-only access to screening results
- Basic reporting capabilities
- Team directory viewing
- Limited export functionality

### Custom Permission Configuration

#### Granular Access Control
Fine-tune permissions for specific needs:
- **Feature-Specific Access**: Grant access to specific modules
- **Data Restrictions**: Limit access to certain data types
- **Workflow Permissions**: Control access to specific screening workflows
- **Reporting Limits**: Restrict sensitive report access

#### Bulk Permission Management
- **Role Templates**: Apply consistent permission sets
- **Batch Updates**: Modify multiple users simultaneously
- **Import/Export**: Manage permissions via spreadsheet
- **Compliance Presets**: Apply regulatory requirement templates
