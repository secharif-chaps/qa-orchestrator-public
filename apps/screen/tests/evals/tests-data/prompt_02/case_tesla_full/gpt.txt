Tesla, Inc. is a leading electric vehicle manufacturer and clean energy company. CEO: Elon Musk (since 2008). Founded: July 1, 2003, by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. Headquarters: Austin, Texas, USA. Employees: ~140,000. Revenue: ~$96.8B (FY2023). Tesla is an independent publicly traded company (NASDAQ: TSLA). The company's mission is to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy.
