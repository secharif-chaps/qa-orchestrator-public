Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. The company designs, develops, and sells consumer electronics, computer software, and online services. Apple is known for products like the iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Apple Watch. The company's CEO is Tim Cook. Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne. Apple employs approximately 164,000 full-time employees and reported revenue of $383.29 billion in fiscal year 2023. Apple's headquarters is located at One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, CA 95014, USA.
