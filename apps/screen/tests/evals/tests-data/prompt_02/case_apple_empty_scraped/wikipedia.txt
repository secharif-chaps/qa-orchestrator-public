Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Apple traces its origins to the personal computer revolution of the 1970s, with Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne founding Apple Computer in 1976. Tim Cook has served as CEO since 2011. Apple had approximately 164,000 full-time employees as of 2023 and generated revenue of $383.29 billion in fiscal year 2023. The company's popular brands include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Apple Watch.
