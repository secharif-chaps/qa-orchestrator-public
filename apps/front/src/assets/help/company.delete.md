# Deleting Company Screenings

Learn how to safely remove company screening records from your workspace while maintaining compliance and audit requirements.

## When to Delete Screenings

### Appropriate Scenarios

- **Duplicate Entries**: Multiple screenings of the same entity
- **Test Screenings**: Development or testing purposes
- **Erroneous Data**: Screenings created with incorrect information
- **Data Retention Compliance**: Removing records per retention policies

### Considerations Before Deletion

- **Regulatory Requirements**: Check if screening records must be retained
- **Audit Trails**: Consider impact on compliance documentation
- **Business Relationships**: Ensure no active business relationships depend on the screening
- **Reference Dependencies**: Verify no other processes reference this screening

## Deletion Process

### Step 1: Access the Screening

1. Navigate to the Companies list
2. Locate the screening you want to delete
3. Click on the screening to open its detail page

### Step 2: Verify Deletion Eligibility

Review the screening for:

- **Current Status**: Ensure no active processes are running
- **Associated Records**: Check for linked business relationships
- **Compliance Requirements**: Verify regulatory retention requirements

### Step 3: Initiate Deletion

1. Click the "Delete" button (trash icon) in the screening actions
2. A confirmation dialog will appear
3. Review the warning message carefully
4. Type the company name to confirm deletion intent
5. Click "Permanently Delete" to complete the action

## Important Warnings

### Data Recovery

⚠️ **Deletion is Permanent**: Once deleted, screening data cannot be recovered
⚠️ **No Backup Restoration**: Deleted records are not included in standard backups
⚠️ **Immediate Effect**: Deletion takes effect immediately across all systems

### Compliance Impact

- **Audit Trails**: Deletion events are logged for compliance purposes
- **Regulatory Reporting**: Ensure deletion doesn't violate reporting requirements
- **Legal Holds**: Never delete records subject to legal preservation orders

## Alternative to Deletion

### Archiving

Consider archiving instead of deleting:

- **Compliance Friendly**: Maintains records for regulatory purposes
- **Reversible**: Can be unarchived if needed
- **Space Efficient**: Reduces active workspace clutter
- **Audit Compliant**: Preserves complete audit trail

### Status Changes

Instead of deletion, consider:

- **Marking as Inactive**: Keep record but mark as no longer relevant
- **Adding Notes**: Document why the screening is no longer valid
- **Status Updates**: Change screening status to reflect current relevance

## Post-Deletion Actions

### Verification

After deletion:

1. **Confirm Removal**: Verify the screening no longer appears in lists
2. **Check Dependencies**: Ensure no broken links or references remain
3. **Update Documentation**: Revise any external documentation that referenced the screening

### Audit Documentation

Maintain records of:

- **Deletion Reason**: Why the screening was removed
- **Authorization**: Who approved the deletion
- **Date and Time**: When the deletion occurred
- **Compliance Check**: Confirmation that deletion met regulatory requirements

## Best Practices

### Before Deleting

- **Document Rationale**: Clear business justification for deletion
- **Obtain Approval**: Get necessary approvals from compliance or management
- **Export Critical Data**: Save important findings before deletion
- **Notify Stakeholders**: Inform relevant team members of the deletion

### Deletion Governance

- **Regular Reviews**: Periodically review screenings for deletion candidates
- **Retention Policies**: Implement clear data retention and deletion policies
- **Role-Based Access**: Limit deletion permissions to authorized personnel
- **Approval Workflows**: Require approval for sensitive or high-value screening deletions
