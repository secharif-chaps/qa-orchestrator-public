# Viewing Company Screenings

Access and navigate company screening lists and detailed screening results to review risk assessments and compliance information.

## Company Screening List

### Overview

The company screening list provides a comprehensive view of all screening tasks within your workspace, allowing you to track progress and review results.

### List Features

#### Screening Status Indicators

- **🟢 Completed**: Screening finished successfully
- **🟡 In Progress**: Analysis currently running
- **🔴 Failed**: Technical issues encountered
- **🟠 Under Review**: Manual review required

#### Filtering and Search

- **Status Filter**: Show screenings by completion status
- **Date Range**: Filter by screening creation or completion dates
- **Risk Level**: Filter by identified risk levels
- **Company Name**: Search for specific companies

#### Sorting Options

- **Creation Date**: Most recent screenings first
- **Completion Date**: Recently completed screenings
- **Risk Level**: High-risk companies first
- **Alphabetical**: Sort by company name

## Individual Screening Page

### Screening Overview

Each screening provides detailed information about the analyzed company:

#### Company Details

- **Basic Information**: Name, registration details, jurisdiction
- **Industry Classification**: Business sector and activity codes
- **Corporate Structure**: Subsidiary and parent company relationships

#### Risk Assessment Summary

- **Overall Risk Score**: Composite risk rating
- **Risk Categories**: Breakdown by risk type
- **Key Findings**: Summary of significant discoveries
- **Compliance Status**: Regulatory compliance assessment

### Detailed Results Sections

#### Sanctions and Watchlist Screening

- **Sanctions Matches**: Any matches against sanctions lists
- **Watchlist Entries**: Regulatory and enforcement watchlists
- **PEP Connections**: Politically Exposed Person associations

#### Financial Analysis

- **Credit Rating**: Current creditworthiness assessment
- **Financial Stability**: Analysis of financial health indicators
- **Payment History**: Historical payment behavior data

#### Adverse Media Analysis

- **Media Coverage**: Relevant news articles and reports
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Overall media sentiment scoring
- **Key Issues**: Significant negative findings

#### Ownership Structure

- **Beneficial Owners**: Ultimate beneficial ownership information
- **Corporate Hierarchy**: Parent and subsidiary relationships
- **Control Structures**: Voting rights and control mechanisms

## Taking Actions

### Available Actions

Depending on your permissions, you can:

- **Export Results**: Download screening reports in various formats
- **Share Screening**: Send screening results to team members
- **Add Notes**: Include internal comments and observations
- **Schedule Re-screening**: Set up periodic screening updates

### Risk-Based Actions

Based on screening results:

- **Approve**: Clear the company for business relationships
- **Reject**: Decline engagement based on risk findings
- **Escalate**: Forward to compliance team for expert review
- **Request Additional Information**: Gather more data before decision

## Understanding Risk Indicators

### Risk Level Interpretation

- **Critical (Red)**: Immediate attention required, high-risk findings
- **High (Orange)**: Significant concerns requiring careful review
- **Medium (Yellow)**: Some concerns, enhanced monitoring recommended
- **Low (Green)**: Minimal risk, standard procedures applicable

### Common Risk Factors

- **Sanctions Exposure**: Direct or indirect sanctions connections
- **PEP Associations**: Connections to politically exposed persons
- **Adverse Media**: Negative news coverage or legal issues
- **Financial Distress**: Poor financial health indicators
- **Regulatory Issues**: Compliance violations or enforcement actions
